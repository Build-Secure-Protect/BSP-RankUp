RankUp Awards and Ladder Chips

Three follow-ups: giving and taking awards on PLAYERS, the rank screen past one row of ladders, and the login line about what a player reached while away (chat only, so questions without a screen).

Each screen is drawn at twice its GUI size with your rank art. PVP, the extra ladders and the three awards are example data. Mrgregles is selected.

Give and take awards on PLAYERS

D101 left awards display-only. These are three ways to give an award to the selected player, or take it away, from the award chips PL-1 drew.

Option A

Pick, then act

A click on an award chip rings it and opens a line under the chips: the award's points, GIVE IT or TAKE IT, and CANCEL. The same habit as SET on a ladder.

For Room to say what happens (no rewards); the same two steps as SET.

Against Two clicks and a line that pushes the counts down while it is open.

Against Each extra row makes the ladder card a row shorter.

Questions

Give and take awards (PLAYERS)

  1. 1. Layout

    Default: A: pick a chip, then GIVE IT or TAKE IT on the line under the chips, as SET does for ladders.

  2. 2. Rewards and announcement on GIVE

    SET RANK moves a rank without rewards or an announcement.

    Default: The same: GIVE adds the LuckPerms group and marks it reached, with no rewards and no announcement. The player is told in chat.

  3. 3. TAKE while their points still reach it

    The next check would give it straight back.

    Default: TAKE marks the award taken for that player: the check never gives it again until an admin gives it back. The chip says TAKEN.

  4. 4. What TAKE does to rewards already given

    Default: Coins, items and pieces stay given. A title the award gave gives way to the next one kept, as at a season end (D75).

  5. 5. Seasonal awards

    A seasonal award is taken back at each season's end and can be reached again.

    Default: A taken award stays taken across seasons until given back.

  6. 6. Commands

    Default: /rankup award give and /rankup award take , the same rules.

  7. 7. Network and version

    The player's row records the taken mark (shared, like awards reached); PLAYERS shows it.

    Default: Ships in 0.5.0 (not yet out); protocol stays 4. Every backend updates together, as before.

The rank screen's ladder chips

  1. 8. Layout

    Default: A: wrap onto more rows, AWARDS last.

  2. 9. The admin LADDER tab

    Its chips stop at the edge the same way (the rest only by /rankup ladders).

    Default: The same rule there: the chips wrap, + LADDER last, and the strip and rungs move down.

  3. 10. Most rows

    Default: Three rows (about 33 ladders); past that the last chip says +n and opens /rankup ladders' list in chat.

Telling a player at login what they reached while away

  1. 11. What it says

    No screen: lines in chat after the "rewards kept for you" line.

    Default: One line per rank or award: "While you were away you reached Captain on Pvp." / "While you were away you earned Headhunter." More than five: the last line says "and 3 more".

  2. 12. Who hears it

    Default: Only the player. Nothing is announced at login (the rank-up was already quiet, D86).

  3. 13. What counts

    Default: What they earned (points, a totem lost). An admin's SET RANK and GIVE stay silent; a rung lost at a season end is not said.

  4. 14. On a network

    Default: Kept in their shared row, so whichever server they join next tells them, once. A player online on another server is told live there (D106) and not again at login.
