BSP Rank Prefix Art

Every image is 9 pixels tall, the height RankUp draws a prefix at (one chat line), so it stays sharp at any GUI scale. Ladder and staff ranks are lettering; the seven achievement ranks are icons. The ranks and colours come from BSP-Luckperms/bsp-ranks.

The chosen style comes first; A, B and C stay below for comparison. Chat and the tab list show the name as decided: staff tag, ladder tag, name, then every achievement held. Two tags and the icons after the name need a RankUp change (task PA-3); the art is ready now.

Decided

  1. D87 Style

    C's swallowtail banners for the ladder and staff ranks; B's full-colour icons, unchanged, on a grey disc.

  2. D88 Two tags

    The staff tag, then the ladder tag, as the LuckPerms format says.

  3. D89 Achievement icons

    Drawn after the name, as images when one is set. PREFIXES gets a SUFFIXES section; hovering shows the text and how it's earned.

  4. D90 Every achievement shows

    Set in LuckPerms' meta-formatting; RankUp draws whatever LuckPerms formats.

  5. D91 Colours and tiers

    LuckPerms' colours, tier metals copper, iron, gold, diamond; dark colours lifted.

  6. D92 Files

    docs/prefix_images/, named after the groups, with commands.txt to assign them.

  7. D93 No image for default

    LuckPerms' built-in group every player has; wanderer is the first rung and has its banner.
