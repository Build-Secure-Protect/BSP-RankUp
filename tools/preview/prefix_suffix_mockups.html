RankUp Suffixes on PREFIXES

PA-3: RankUp starts drawing LuckPerms suffixes (the seven achievements, after the name), and shows the staff tag and the ladder tag together. Images come from what LuckPerms formats (D88 to D90). These are three ways to manage it on the PREFIXES tab.

Each screen is the admin screen at twice its GUI size with your new art. Totem Reaver is selected, and the tooltip shows the new hover note.

Option A

One list, a suffix section

Today's PREFIXES, with a new section at the end of the list for what comes after the name. The editor adds a HOVER NOTE, and IN CHAT shows a whole line with the chosen piece ringed.

For Smallest change; one place to look.

Against The list gets long (25 rows with headers), so the suffixes sit below a scroll.

Questions

  1. 1. Layout

    A, B or C, or parts combined.

    Default: A: the smallest change, and it keeps one list.

  2. 2. How RankUp reads the name line

    LuckPerms gives RankUp one formatted prefix ("[Admin] [Legend] ") and one suffix (" ✦ Champion ✦ Founder"). RankUp splits them into the prefixes and suffixes it knows: each group's own and RankUp's titles. Each known piece is drawn as its image; anything else stays text.

    Default: Yes. Whatever you set in LuckPerms' meta-formatting is what shows.

  3. 3. Hover note

    D89 says hovering an icon shows how it's earned, but LuckPerms has nowhere to keep that, so RankUp keeps a note per tag. It's typed on PREFIXES and shared through the database.

    Default: Yes, on suffixes and prefixes alike. commands.txt fills the seven achievement notes from the bsp-ranks table.

  4. 4. Spacing

    LuckPerms puts a space between pieces. Between two images that's 4 pixels.

    Default: 1 pixel between two images (as D68), a normal space next to text and on each side of the name.

  5. 5. Commands

    Default: /rankup prefix image|clear suffix , /rankup prefix note prefix|suffix , and prefix list shows suffixes.

  6. 6. The OPTIONS switch

    "Show prefixes" today.

    Default: One switch for both, renamed "Show prefixes and suffixes".

  7. 7. Version

    The admin packets gain suffix rows and notes, so the protocol changes.

    Default: Protocol 4 and version 0.5.0: the server and the modpack update together.

  8. 8. The rank screen

    The player's own rank screen shows titles per ladder.

    Default: Unchanged for now; achievements already show on AWARDS when they're RankUp awards.
