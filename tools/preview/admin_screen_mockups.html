RankUp admin screen: three options

Each mock-up is one Minecraft screen at GUI scale 2 (856 by 480 pixels) in BSP Core's Holo HUD style, the look Region Guard's screens already use. The same example ladder appears in all three, five ranks on the LuckPerms track rankup: Settler (0 points), Raider (500), Warden (1 500), Vanguard (4 000, seasonal) and Legend (10 000, seasonal); the same signals and their points; the same options. Everything the mod does is set here (decision D11): the config file keeps only the database connection (D19). Pick one, or name the parts you want combined.

A. Ledger four tabs, a list on the left, the chosen rank on the right

Region Guard's shape. Tabs in the header: LADDER, SIGNALS, PLAYERS, OPTIONS. On LADDER the ranks are a list in track order with their points and a seasonal pill; the chosen rank's fields sit on the right: group, display name, points needed, permanent or seasonal, and its rewards as typed rows (coins, pieces, item, title, command) with ADD REWARD and FROM HAND (adds the stack you hold). SIGNALS is a table of every signal with on/off and points each; PLAYERS a search with set, add and reset; OPTIONS the track name, announcements, titles and the storage lines.

Good at: many ranks and many rewards; one tab per job keeps each screen simple; the fastest to build, since every piece has a Region Guard twin. Weak at: seeing the ladder as a whole while you edit one rank; signals and ranks are never on screen together.

B. Ladder board the track drawn down the left, everything else edits on the right

The ladder is the navigation. Down the left the ranks stand on a drawn track from Settler at the foot to Legend at the top, each a card with its points, seasonal mark and how many players hold it; the lit part of the track shows how far most players have come. Below the foot sits SIGNALS, above the top OPTIONS, so one column holds the whole mod. Click anything and the right pane becomes its editor; the rank editor is A's. PLAYERS is a search box in the header that takes the right pane over while you type.

Good at: seeing the shape of the ladder while editing one rung, which is what a ranking mod is about; signals and the ladder on one screen; the player counts per rank tell you at once whether thresholds are right. Weak at: more than seven ranks need the column to scroll; a little more drawing to build than A.

C. Dashboard four panes at a glance, each opens full size

One screen shows the state of the whole mod: LADDER as a compact table, SIGNALS with their points as bars, PLAYERS with a search and the latest rank-ups across the network, OPTIONS with the switches and the storage health. Nothing is edited in the small panes; clicking a pane's title opens it full size (the ladder then looks like A's LADDER tab) with BACK in the corner.

Good at: the admin's first question, "is it working?": the feed and the health lines answer it without a click; good when you open the screen to look rather than to edit. Weak at: every edit is two clicks away; four dense panes are small at GUI scale 2; the most to build, since each pane has a small and a full form.

Decisions, with defaults

Answer by number in chat; "default" takes the one in gold. The answers go in RankUp - Design Decisions.

  1. Layout. A ledger with tabs, B ladder board, C dashboard, or parts combined. Default: B, with C's "latest rank-ups" feed shown in B's PLAYERS pane.
  2. How it opens. A command only, or also an item or key. Default: /rankup admin, for BSP Core admins (the same admin list as the other mods).
  3. Reward rows. Five kinds: coins (Shatter Coins into the hand), pieces (bank), item (an id and count, or FROM HAND), title (a LuckPerms prefix), command (run by the console with %player%). Default: all five, any number per rank.
  4. Destructive buttons. REMOVE RANK and RESET PLAYER: ask again or not. Default: the button turns red and says CLICK AGAIN for 5 seconds; RESET EVERYONE (in OPTIONS) uses Region Guard's typed code.
  5. Players pane. Search by name, including players not online and players last seen on another server; shows rank, points per signal and when they ranked up; SET RANK, ADD POINTS, RESET. Default: yes, all of it.
  6. Points per rank or per signal. One total per rank (D5), or also a minimum per signal (for example "at least 1 totem stolen for Warden"). Default: the total only; per-signal minimums later if wanted.
  7. Make the track in LuckPerms. A button in OPTIONS that creates the track and any missing groups from this ladder (the D2 option). Default: present, behind the CLICK AGAIN confirmation.
  8. Screen size. 428 by 240 GUI pixels as drawn (Region Guard's size), or BSP Core's smaller 360 by 206. Default: 428 by 240.
  9. Saving. Every change saves at once and reaches every server, or a SAVE button. Default: at once, as Region Guard does.
  10. What the player screen gets. Decided in its own mock-ups next, after this pick. Default: the player screen reuses B's ladder drawing with the player's own position lit.
