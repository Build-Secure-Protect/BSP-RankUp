Several ladders and a GROUPS tab: three options

Decisions D49 and D50. Each option is the admin screen at its real size (520 by 300 GUI units, drawn here at 1.6 pixels a unit), shown on the tab for ladders and on the new GROUPS tab. The same example data is in all three:

Ladder Playtime on LuckPerms track rankup, counting minutes played only: Wanderer 0, Settler 120, Recruit 300, Scout 600, Sentinel 1 200, Warden 2 100 and on to Legend 21 000 (12 rungs).

Ladder Raider on track raider, counting player kills, totems stolen, totems protected: Cutpurse 150, Raider 750, Reaver 3 000.

Award apple_picker: on no ladder, counting pieces earned, needs 5 000 points (5 000 pieces at 1 point a piece). When a player reaches it, RankUp adds the group to their LuckPerms profile, and it stays.

LuckPerms' other groups (admin, staff, moderator, founder, season_champion and so on) show in GROUPS too; RankUp leaves them alone until one is made an award.

A. Ladder chips a row of ladders on the LADDER tab

How it works. The LADDER tab gains a row of ladder chips at the top; click one to edit it, + LADDER to add one. Under the chips, the ladder's track and the signals it counts, as chips you switch on and off. The rung list and the rung editor below are today's. GROUPS is a new second tab: every LuckPerms group on the left in three parts (on a ladder, awards, not RankUp's), the chosen group's rule on the right.

Trade-off. The closest to today's screen, so the least to learn. The chips row fits about six ladders before it scrolls. The rung list loses two rows to the ladder strip.

B. Three columns ladders, rungs, editor side by side

How it works. The tab becomes LADDERS: a narrow column of ladders on the left (each with its track and what it counts), the chosen ladder's rungs in the middle, the rung editor on the right. GROUPS is one table across the screen (group, what it is, what counts, what it needs, how many hold it); the chosen row's editor opens under the table. A group on a ladder shows its ladder's rule read-only, with a button to jump to it.

Trade-off. Every ladder in view at once, and the GROUPS table reads at a glance. The rung editor is narrower, so rewards show fewer at a time and long reward names are cut sooner.

C. Signals grid what counts, set in one place

How it works. The SIGNALS tab becomes a grid: one row per signal (its points, as today), one column per ladder and one for awards; a tick says the signal counts there. The LADDER tab picks its ladder from a drop-down at the top and is otherwise today's. GROUPS shows every group as a card; an award's card opens its editor.

Trade-off. Easiest to see which signal feeds which ladder. An award's signals are set on its card, not in the grid, because each award has its own. Two places to look instead of one.

Pick one, and the open questions

Each question has a default in gold; say only where you want something else. The player's rank screen gets its own three mock-ups next, once the admin layout is chosen.

  1. Layout: A, B or C, or parts combined. Default: A.
  2. Today's ladder becomes the first ladder, named after its track, counting all signals as now; nothing changes until you edit it. Default: yes.
  3. Announcements for every ladder, like today (off, server, network is one setting for all). Default: one setting for all ladders.
  4. Titles: a TITLE reward sets a LuckPerms prefix at the title priority; LuckPerms shows the highest. Each ladder gets its own priority so they do not overwrite each other (first ladder 1000, then 999, 998). Default: yes.
  5. Seasons stay per rung (keep or reset), on every ladder. Default: yes.
  6. Awards stay once reached (no demotion, as D6); an award can be marked to reset each season like a rung. Default: yes.
  7. Awards give rewards with the same kinds as rungs (coins, pieces, item, title, command, kit). Default: yes.
  8. A group on a ladder is not also an award; GROUPS shows its ladder's rule and sends you to the ladder to change it. Default: yes.
  9. Importing groups: GROUPS reads LuckPerms' groups each time it opens, with a REFRESH button; nothing is created in LuckPerms. A group deleted in LuckPerms keeps its rule, marked missing. Default: yes.
  10. A player who already has an award's group (given by hand): RankUp marks it reached and gives no rewards. Default: yes.
  11. BSP-Core's kits keep reading the first ladder for their rank rules; a request to BSP-Core for choosing a ladder per kit later. Default: yes.
  12. Updating: the screens' packets change, so the server and the players' modpack must update together. Default: accepted.
