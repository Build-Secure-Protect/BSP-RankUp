Which signals a ladder counts: three options

Today the LADDER tab's strip lists a ladder's signals as small chips after COUNTS ("minutes played" is the playtime signal), and an award shows them in GROUPS. Nothing shows the whole picture: which signal feeds which ladder or award, and what it is worth there. Example data in all three: ladder Rankup counts minutes played (1 point a minute); ladder Raider counts player kills and totems stolen; the award apple_picker counts pieces earned.

A. A "progress from" line on LADDER and GROUPS

How it works. The ladder's strip becomes two lines. The second, PROGRESS FROM, lists each counted signal as a bright chip with its worth ("minutes played, 1 pt a minute"); a click on its x takes it off, + SIGNAL adds one. The rung's POINTS field says what the points mean ("= 120 minutes played") when the ladder counts one signal. An award on GROUPS gets the same line.

Trade-off. The answer sits right where you edit the ladder, in words. The rung list loses one row (12 visible, which still fits your 12 rungs; more scroll). You still look ladder by ladder.

B. A matrix on SIGNALS every ladder and award at once

How it works. SIGNALS gets a column per ladder and per award. A tick means that signal counts there; click a cell to switch it. The worth column stays as it is. LADDER and GROUPS keep their chips, drawn brighter.

Trade-off. The best overview, on one screen. With many ladders and awards the columns run out (about six fit; the rest scroll sideways). You go to a different tab to see it.

SIGNALS

C. Signals on the ladder chips and on every group row

How it works. Each ladder chip at the top grows a second line naming what it counts ("minutes played", "kills, totems stolen"), so the ladder row itself is the overview. On GROUPS every row shows its signals, for rungs (from their ladder) and awards alike.

Trade-off. Always in view, no extra click. Signal names are cut short when a ladder counts many; the chip row is taller, so the rung list loses one row; the worth is not shown.

Pick one, and the open questions

Each question has a default in gold; say only where you want something else.

  1. Layout: A, B or C, or parts combined. Default: A and B together: the "progress from" line where you edit, the matrix for the overview.
  2. Signal names: the screen's names ("minutes played") with the command's key on hover ("playtime"), so typing a key in + SIGNAL is easy to match. Default: yes.
  3. A ladder counting every signal says "every signal" in gold, so it is not mistaken for a ladder counting nothing. Default: yes.
  4. Signals that are off (SIGNALS tab) but counted by a ladder show dimmed with "off: worth nothing now". Default: yes.
  5. The player's rank screen already lists only the chosen ladder's signals under WHAT COUNTS; no change there. Default: no change.
