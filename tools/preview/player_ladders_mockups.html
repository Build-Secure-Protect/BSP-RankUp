The player's rank screen with several ladders: three options

Decisions D49 to D51. Each option is the rank screen players open with /rank, the U key or the inventory button, at its real size (520 by 300 GUI units). The same example player is in all three:

Playtime ladder (counts minutes played): on Sentinel, rung 5 of 12, with 1 380 points; next is Warden at 2 100 (720 minutes, 12 hours, to go).

Raider ladder (counts player kills, totems stolen and protected): on Cutpurse, rung 1 of 3, with 420 points; next is Raider at 750.

Awards: apple_picker reached (5 000 pieces earned); bulwark at 720 of 1 500 points (12 of 25 totems protected).

A. Ladder chips like the admin screen

How it works. A row of chips under the title: one per ladder, then AWARDS. A ladder chip shows today's screen for that ladder: its rungs on the track at the left, the player's standing, the bar to the next rung and what counts for this ladder only. The AWARDS chip turns the screen into a list of every award, reached or not, with a bar each.

Trade-off. Matches the admin layout you picked, and each ladder keeps the whole screen. A player sees one ladder at a time, so their place on the other ladders is one click away.

B. Overview first every ladder at a glance

How it works. The screen opens on an overview: one wide card per ladder (the rung held, the bar to the next, what it counts) and a row of award badges under them. Clicking a card opens today's detailed screen for that ladder, with a BACK button to the overview.

Trade-off. The whole picture in one look, which suits players on several ladders. The ladder's full track and the table of what counts are one click deeper. With more than four ladders, the cards scroll.

C. Tracks side by side all ladders on the left

How it works. The left of the screen holds one narrow track per ladder, side by side, each with its rung names and the player's rung in gold. Clicking a track chooses it; the right shows that ladder's standing and what counts, as today. The awards sit as badges along the bottom of the right side.

Trade-off. Every ladder and every award stay in view while reading one ladder's detail. The tracks are narrow, so rung names are cut sooner, and three ladders is about the most that fits before they scroll sideways.

Pick one, and the open questions

Each question has a default in gold; say only where you want something else.

  1. Layout: A, B or C, or parts combined. Default: A, to match the admin screen.
  2. Which ladder opens first: the one the player looked at last, else the first ladder. Default: yes.
  3. What counts lists only the chosen ladder's signals (with their counts, worth and points toward that ladder). Default: yes.
  4. Awards shown: every award, reached ones marked, the rest with their bar; an award a player has no progress toward still shows, at 0. Default: show every award.
  5. Title chip: shows the title LuckPerms displays (the highest priority across ladders and awards). Default: yes.
  6. Hovering a rung or an award shows what it gives at the bottom, as today. Default: yes.
  7. /rank in chat (the text version for /rank <player>) lists each ladder on its own line, then awards reached. Default: yes.
  8. Rank-up message names the ladder when there is more than one ("You are now Warden on Playtime!"). Default: yes.
