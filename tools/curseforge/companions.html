BSP CORE (required)

Totem events: steals that succeed count as stolen for the thief and lost for the owner, steals that fail while you are online as protected, totems placed. The bank, the coins and the kits for rewards, the season score as a signal, the teams. The same admin list as every BSP mod. Season ends drive the seasonal rungs. Needs 1.4.5 or newer.

BSP REGION GUARD (optional)

Buying a plot at the Land Office counts a claim bought. Region Guard's regions and mines are gated by LuckPerms groups: the ranks and awards RankUp hands out open them (list every rung from the lowest that should get in). The server id is shared.

LUCKPERMS and BSP TELEPORT

LuckPerms holds the ranks (the Forge mod, or the Bukkit plugin on Mohist): RankUp moves players along each ladder's track, adds award groups, sets titles, and shows every player's prefix before their name, as an image when the rank has one. BSP Teleport needs no link.
